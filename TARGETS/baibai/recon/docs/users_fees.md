# Fees

> BaiBai is designed to minimize the total cost of every trade.

## Gas Fees

**No gas fees are required.**

BaiBai supports gasless trading, so you don't need ETH to pay network fees. Simply sign your trade, and the network fee is handled by the platform.

## Trading Fees

There is currently a 10 bps trading fee on BaiBai. The fee is designed so that users continue to receive better overall execution than other trading venues, even after fees.

Our goal is simple:

> **Everything we build is designed to deliver the best prices onchain.**
