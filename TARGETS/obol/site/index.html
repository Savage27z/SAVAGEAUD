The daemon

Its own wallet. Every daemon has an on-chain wallet (ERC-6551) that follows the NFT. 90% of the price is paid into it in the same transaction; 10% to SHYGUY LLC. The sale contract holds nothing between purchases.

Its look, revealed when it sells. Top, colours, shoes, casing, phosphor, face, hat — fixed and committed before the mint. Until a number sells it shows the same unrevealed spin, so nobody can pick the rare ones.

Its screen. Wallet, log, inbox, repo, market — live. Activation opens 24 hours after the sale (Sun 11 Oct, 18:00 UTC). Autonomy follows.
